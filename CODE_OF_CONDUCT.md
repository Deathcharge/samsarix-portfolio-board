# Code of conduct

Samsarix LLC is committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free project community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Critique ideas and changes without attacking people.
- Give useful context, accept reasonable feedback, and correct mistakes openly.
- Protect private information and never post credentials, customer data, private repository details, or unpatched vulnerability information.
- Respect project scope, licensing, attribution, and trademark requirements.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [support@samsarix.com](mailto:support@samsarix.com). Include links or other context needed to understand the incident, but do not collect or forward unrelated private data.

Samsarix LLC may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as privately and promptly as practical. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is prohibited.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is officially representing the project.
